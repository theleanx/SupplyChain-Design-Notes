# Section B: Risk Analysis and Response

Section B applies the risk definitions, governance and register established in Section A to **classification, qualitative and quantitative assessment, response selection, protective action, and tested continuity**. All applications use original fictional scenarios for Stonebridge Industrial Components.

## Learning outcomes

- Separate internal/external event causes from shared dependencies and downstream damage.
- Calibrate qualitative ratings, urgency and evidence quality before estimating event probabilities.
- Calculate EMV, fixed-cost and residual-loss economics, decision sensitivity and simulation summaries on a consistent horizon.
- Distinguish accept, avoid, transfer and mitigate, and apply mandatory gates before economics.
- Build ready-to-activate plans, operational contingencies, partner communication and evidence-based continuity exercises.

## How to study the section

Start with **classification and urgency (B01–B03)**, then introduce reliable probability and impact inputs (B04–B08). Use **response choices and operational action plans (B09–B11)** before studying category-specific interventions (B12–B18). End by testing whole-system continuity (B19) and checking the rationale of each answer in the **original review (B20)**.

Only eligible alternatives are compared financially. Signed EMV and positive loss magnitudes are not interchangeable. Certain annual control fees are not multiplied by event probability. All example thresholds and probabilities are Stonebridge learning assumptions and are not real-world event statistics.
