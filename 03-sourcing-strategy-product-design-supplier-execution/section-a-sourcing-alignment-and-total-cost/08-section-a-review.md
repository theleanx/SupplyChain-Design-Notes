# 8. Section A Review

Use these independently authored questions to test decision logic, not term recall. Choose an answer before expanding the rationale.

## Question 1

**Question.** A make-or-buy model compares supplier price only with internal variable cost. What is missing?

A. Only the supplier's logo

B. Relevant internal fixed, transition, risk, capacity, and lifecycle consequences

C. The buyer's job title

D. The prior purchase-order number

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Relevant internal fixed, transition, risk, capacity, and lifecycle consequences**

### Why it is correct

The alternatives need comparable full-scope economics and strategic consequences.

### Why the other answers are wrong

A, C, and D do not correct the decision boundary.

</details>

## Question 2

**Question.** Which option is outsourcing without offshoring?

A. An owned plant abroad

B. A domestic external manufacturer

C. An internal local cell

D. An owned regional warehouse

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. A domestic external manufacturer**

### Why it is correct

External ownership changes while the work remains in the same country.

### Why the other answers are wrong

A changes location without ownership; C and D remain internal.

</details>

## Question 5

**Question.** What is the correct cost nesting?

A. TCO inside price

B. Price inside landed cost inside total cost

C. Landed cost inside price

D. All three are identical

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Price inside landed cost inside total cost**

### Why it is correct

Landed cost extends price; total cost extends landed cost through the decision horizon.

### Why the other answers are wrong

A, C, and D reverse or erase the scopes.

</details>

## Question 6

**Question.** When should risk estimates remain separate from hard cost?

A. When their basis is too uncertain for responsible monetization

B. Never

C. Only when the supplier is local

D. Only after award

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**A. When their basis is too uncertain for responsible monetization**

### Why it is correct

Separating uncertain exposure prevents false precision while keeping risk visible.

### Why the other answers are wrong

B, C, and D use irrelevant rules.

</details>
