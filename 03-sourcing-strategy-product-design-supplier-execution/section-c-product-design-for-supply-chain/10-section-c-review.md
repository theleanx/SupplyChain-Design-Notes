Use these independently authored questions to test decision logic, not term recall. Choose an answer before expanding the rationale.

## Question 1

**Question.** A defect-free product does not meet customer needs. What failed?

A. Conformance only

B. Design quality

C. Invoice matching

D. Bid opening

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Design quality**

### Why it is correct

The product consistently delivers the wrong value proposition.

### Why the other answers are wrong

A is strong in the scenario; C and D are unrelated.

</details>

## Question 2

**Question.** What is the main advantage of early cross-functional involvement?

A. Problems are found while change is less costly

B. It guarantees no trade-offs

C. It removes design ownership

D. It eliminates testing

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**A. Problems are found while change is less costly**

### Why it is correct

Early evidence preserves design options.

### Why the other answers are wrong

B, C, and D overstate or weaken governance.

</details>

## Question 4

**Question.** What is component commonality?

A. Using one qualified part across multiple applications

B. Creating a unique part for every model

C. Moving final assembly

D. Changing supplier ownership

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**A. Using one qualified part across multiple applications**

### Why it is correct

Commonality deliberately reuses a component.

### Why the other answers are wrong

B is the opposite; C and D are different decisions.

</details>
