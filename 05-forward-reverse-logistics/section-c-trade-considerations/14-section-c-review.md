## Question 1

**Question.** A planner wants to ship despite an unresolved product restriction check because the truck is waiting. What is the strongest control?

A. Keep the compliance hold until the restriction is resolved and approved

B. Ask the trucker to accept the risk

C. Change the freight mode

D. Use a different invoice template

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**A. Keep the compliance hold until the restriction is resolved and approved**

### Why it is correct

A shipment-release gate must not be bypassed by transportation urgency.

### Why the other answers are wrong

The other choices do not satisfy the controlling trade decision. Specifically: B (Ask the trucker to accept the risk), C (Change the freight mode), D (Use a different invoice template).
