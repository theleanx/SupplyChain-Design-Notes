## Freshness rule

A URL or familiar program name is not proof that a rule is unchanged. For a real shipment or waste movement, record the authority, jurisdiction, page or legal text consulted, retrieval date, applicable version/revision, responsible reviewer, and transaction facts. Obtain qualified legal, customs, tax, labor, safety, environmental, or dangerous-goods advice where appropriate.
