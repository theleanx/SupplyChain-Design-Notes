# 15. Section A Review

Use these independently authored questions to test customer relationship management decisions. Choose an answer before opening the rationale.

## Question 1

**Question.** Why is CRM not equivalent to CRM software?

A. Because CRM is only a marketing budget

B. Because CRM is a relationship operating philosophy that technology may enable

C. Because CRM never uses customer data

D. Because CRM applies only after a customer churns

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Because CRM is a relationship operating philosophy that technology may enable**

### Why it is correct

The relationship model includes customer evidence, ownership, coordinated action, service outcomes, and learning; software can support but cannot replace those decisions.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** CRM spans more than budget management.

- **C.** Customer data is a core input to CRM.

- **D.** CRM covers prospects, active, vulnerable, loyal, and churned relationships.

</details>

## Question 2

**Question.** Which segmentation variable is most useful in Module 6?

A. Any attribute that produces the largest number of segments

B. A variable that changes relationship treatment, service intensity, or contact approach

C. Only annual revenue

D. Only customer geography

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. A variable that changes relationship treatment, service intensity, or contact approach**

### Why it is correct

Module 6 segmentation is useful when it changes how the relationship is managed.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** Granularity without a decision creates complexity.

- **C.** Revenue alone can miss needs, margin, service criticality, and channel preference.

- **D.** Geography can matter but is not universally sufficient.

</details>

## Question 3

**Question.** What is the safest interpretation of a Pareto pattern in customer value?

A. Exactly 20% of customers must produce 80% of profit

B. Value may be concentrated, so measure the actual portfolio before prioritizing relationship investment

C. The bottom 80% should be discontinued

D. Pareto replaces profitability analysis

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Value may be concentrated, so measure the actual portfolio before prioritizing relationship investment**

### Why it is correct

Pareto is a concentration lens, not a fixed mathematical rule for every portfolio.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** The exact split is not guaranteed.

- **C.** Lower-value customers still require a viable service proposition.

- **D.** Profitability and other relationship evidence remain necessary.

</details>

## Question 4

**Question.** A segment values convenience and self-service, while another values engineering coordination. What should the CRM team do?

A. Give both the same contact model

B. Translate the different value profiles into different relationship service propositions

C. Redesign the factory first

D. Ignore stated needs and use only internal cost

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Translate the different value profiles into different relationship service propositions**

### Why it is correct

The value profile exists to convert customer needs into an appropriate relationship treatment.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** Uniform treatment can misfit both groups.

- **C.** Factory design does not answer the relationship-service need.

- **D.** Cost matters, but the treatment must also fit customer value.

</details>

## Question 5

**Question.** What is the distinction between a contact channel and a fulfillment strategy?

A. There is no distinction

B. A contact channel manages communication/service interaction; fulfillment defines how the physical order is executed

C. Contact channel is always a warehouse

D. Fulfillment is always an email

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. A contact channel manages communication/service interaction; fulfillment defines how the physical order is executed**

### Why it is correct

Module 6 owns the relationship/contact path; Module 5 owns physical order fulfillment.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** The decisions can use the same technology but have different purposes.

- **C.** A warehouse is a physical node, not a contact channel.

- **D.** Fulfillment is a physical/logistics design, not a communication medium.

</details>

## Question 6

**Question.** A customer complaint conflicts with an apparently good operational KPI. What is the best next step?

A. Discard the complaint

B. Combine VOC with operational and interaction evidence to diagnose the relationship issue

C. Assume the KPI is wrong

D. Replace the CRM system

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Combine VOC with operational and interaction evidence to diagnose the relationship issue**

### Why it is correct

Direct customer feedback and internal service measures answer different questions and should be reconciled.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** The complaint may expose an issue not captured by the KPI.

- **C.** The KPI may be correct but incomplete.

- **D.** Technology replacement is premature before diagnosis.

</details>

## Question 7

**Question.** What converts a customer segment into a CRM segment strategy?

A. A segment label alone

B. A defined relationship objective, service intensity, channel, ownership, triggers, and measures

C. A product price only

D. A larger marketing budget

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. A defined relationship objective, service intensity, channel, ownership, triggers, and measures**

### Why it is correct

The strategy is the differentiated treatment that follows from the segment.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** A label without a changed decision creates little value.

- **C.** Price is one possible commercial lever, not the whole relationship strategy.

- **D.** Budget does not define the operating treatment.

</details>

## Question 8

**Question.** Which account belongs in the denominator for the repository-defined churn calculation?

A. A new account acquired halfway through the period

B. An account active at the start of the period

C. Every prospect in the CRM database

D. Only accounts with complaints

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. An account active at the start of the period**

### Why it is correct

The locked training rule uses the starting active-account population as the denominator.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** New accounts were not present at the denominator date.

- **C.** Prospects are not starting active customers.

- **D.** Complaint status is not the denominator rule.

</details>

## Question 9

**Question.** What is the strongest lifetime-customer principle?

A. Retain every customer at any cost

B. Seek sustainable long-run mutual value and diagnose why good-fit customers weaken or leave

C. Use tenure as proof of profitability

D. Use one universal CLV formula for all companies

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Seek sustainable long-run mutual value and diagnose why good-fit customers weaken or leave**

### Why it is correct

A lifetime view values retention and learning while still requiring sound economics and fit.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** Unprofitable or poor-fit relationships may need redesign or exit.

- **C.** Tenure does not prove profitability.

- **D.** No single CLV formula is assumed as universal in this repository.

</details>

## Question 10

**Question.** A recurring customer issue crosses sales, service, planning, and logistics. Which control is most important?

A. Four independent task owners with no accountable relationship owner

B. One accountable relationship-process owner with explicit cross-functional roles

C. Move the issue to IT

D. Ask the customer to coordinate the functions

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. One accountable relationship-process owner with explicit cross-functional roles**

### Why it is correct

Cross-functional execution requires one coherent customer outcome and accountable ownership.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** Independent closure can leave the end-to-end issue unresolved.

- **C.** IT may enable the process but does not own the business relationship decision.

- **D.** The supplier should coordinate its own internal organization.

</details>

## Question 11

**Question.** A CRM solution cannot access required order-status data. What should happen before economic ranking?

A. Ignore the gap because order status lives elsewhere

B. Treat data access as a feasibility problem

C. Add more marketing automation

D. Choose the lowest subscription price

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Treat data access as a feasibility problem**

### Why it is correct

A mandatory relationship process cannot work without required evidence; feasibility precedes ranking.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** CRM can consume data from another authoritative source.

- **C.** More automation does not repair missing required information.

- **D.** Low price cannot compensate for a failed capability gate.

</details>

## Question 12

**Question.** Which task is the best candidate for full automation?

A. A routine low-risk onboarding reminder with stable rules

B. Compensation after a critical service failure

C. Renegotiating a strategic commitment

D. Determining whether a disputed churned account should be won back

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**A. A routine low-risk onboarding reminder with stable rules**

### Why it is correct

Stable, repeatable, low-risk tasks are the strongest automation candidates.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **B.** Sensitive recovery requires human judgment.

- **C.** Negotiation requires human decision rights.

- **D.** A disputed relationship state requires investigation and judgment.

</details>

## Question 13

**Question.** A CRM system is live but users maintain the real account process in spreadsheets. What does this indicate?

A. Successful implementation

B. An adoption/process-design problem

C. The need to eliminate customer feedback

D. A product life-cycle issue

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. An adoption/process-design problem**

### Why it is correct

Technical go-live does not create value if users do not adopt the governed relationship process.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** Availability is only a milestone.

- **C.** Customer feedback remains necessary.

- **D.** The behavior points to workflow/adoption rather than life-cycle stage.

</details>

## Question 14

**Question.** Why can on-time delivery be higher than OTIF?

A. OTIF ignores timeliness

B. OTIF requires both completeness and timeliness, so timely but incomplete orders fail OTIF

C. On-time delivery measures customer satisfaction

D. The percentages must always be equal

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. OTIF requires both completeness and timeliness, so timely but incomplete orders fail OTIF**

### Why it is correct

OTIF is the stricter conjunction of conditions.

### Why the other answers are wrong

- **A.** Timeliness is part of OTIF.

- **C.** On-time delivery is an operational measure, not satisfaction.

- **D.** Different definitions can legitimately produce different results.

</details>
