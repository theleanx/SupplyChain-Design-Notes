# Section A: Customer Relationships

Section A develops the customer-side relationship operating model for Module 6. It moves from customer-centric CRM purpose through segmentation, value, service design, contact channels, VOC and customer information, differentiated CRM strategies, relationship lifecycle, retention economics, process ownership, CRM technology and automation, implementation, and customer-service feedback/recovery. The recurring fictional organization is **ClearSpan Automation Systems**. All customers, values, datasets, visuals, calculations, and questions are independently created for learning.

## Learning outcomes

After completing Section A, you should be able to:

- explain CRM as a customer-first relationship philosophy and operating model rather than only a software platform;
- segment customers and translate value, needs, behavior, and channel preference into differentiated relationship treatment;
- manage prospects, vulnerable accounts, loyal customers, churn, retention, and win-back using evidence;
- design CRM process ownership, technology capability, and automation boundaries around customer outcomes;
- interpret customer service metrics together with direct customer feedback and service recovery; and
- connect the customer relationship back to Modules 1–5 without duplicating demand, technology architecture, sourcing, operations, or logistics content.

## How to study the section

Follow one relationship logic through the section: **understand the customer → segment only where treatment changes → define value and service needs → choose the right contact and CRM strategy → manage relationship state → assign process ownership → enable the process with technology → measure service and direct feedback → recover and learn**. When a lesson needs demand management, enterprise architecture, metric-system design, product design, or fulfillment mechanics, use the linked Modules 1–5 lesson as an input rather than rebuilding it here.
