# Section B — Sustainability Primary Sources and Edition Controls

The **privately supplied 2026 APICS CSCP Learning System, Module 8, Section B** establishes the coverage taxonomy, but the repository content, graphs, cases and questions are original. This register does not distribute APICS-owned PDFs, presentation slides, flashcards or proprietary examination wording.

## Limitations

- The fictional transport factors, cost estimates, labor incidents and evidence-coverage fractions are **training assumptions**, not official external sustainability scores.
- Responsible sourcing and reporting claims must identify applicable entity, jurisdiction, product, relevant date, affected workers and credible evidence.
- Use full edition-specific references and verify any changed official standard before actual practice.
