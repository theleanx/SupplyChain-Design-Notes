# 10. Section C Review and Original Practice

These original questions reinforce the section's concepts through practical scenarios and decision contexts.

## Rapid review

Remember these core distinctions:

- **Forecast** = estimate of what may happen.
- **Demand plan** = consensus plan the organization intends to support and help realize.
- **Plan demand** = forecast + assumptions + commercial commitments.
- **Communicate demand** = move useful information early, structurally, and to the right audience.
- **Influence demand** = change timing, mix, volume, channel, or customer behavior.
- **Prioritize demand** = decide how scarce supply should be allocated.
- **Order qualifier** = required to be considered.
- **Order winner** = reason a customer chooses you.
- **PDCA** = plan, execute, measure, learn, adapt.
- **Four Ps** = product, price, place, promotion.
- **PLC** = stages a product moves through.
- **PLM** = management and information discipline across those stages.
- **High demand uncertainty** generally pushes the supply chain toward more pull/responsiveness.

## Question 1

A manufacturer creates a statistical forecast of 40,000 units. Sales then commits to a promotion expected to add 6,000 units, while product management plans to retire an old variant that currently contributes 3,000 units. What should the organization build next?

A. Only the 40,000-unit statistical forecast  
B. A consensus demand plan reflecting the forecast, commercial actions, and assumptions  
C. A production schedule immediately, without further review  
D. A financial target that replaces the forecast  

**Answer: B**

**Why:** Demand planning goes beyond the statistical forecast. The demand plan should incorporate planned actions and assumptions before supply commits resources.

---

## Question 2

A company competes in a regulated market. Certification compliance is required before a customer will even consider a supplier. Which concept best fits certification compliance?

A. Order winner  
B. Order qualifier  
C. Demand shaper  
D. Promotional channel  

**Answer: B**

**Why:** A qualifier is the minimum condition to compete. It may not be the reason the customer ultimately chooses the supplier.

---

## Question 3

A distributor tells a manufacturer about a major campaign only three days before it begins because the expected uplift was uncertain. Which communication principle was violated most directly?

A. Focus communications  
B. Communicate soon  
C. Product life-cycle management  
D. Contribution margin  

**Answer: B**

**Why:** Uncertain information can still be shared early as a range with assumptions. Delay removes response options.

---

## Question 4

Operations receives demand information in units while finance receives the same plan in revenue and margin. Which communication principle does this illustrate?

A. Focus communications  
B. Manage demand  
C. Communicate only after consensus  
D. Demand generation  

**Answer: A**

**Why:** The same underlying demand must be presented in the form most useful to the audience.

## Question 5

Who is best positioned to gather demand information, analyze it, build consensus, communicate it across functions, and coordinate feedback?

A. Master scheduler  
B. Demand manager  
C. Warehouse supervisor  
D. Supplier quality engineer  

**Answer: B**

**Why:** Those are core demand-manager responsibilities.

## Question 6

A marketing campaign falls short. The team compares actual volume with the target, identifies that the message reached the wrong segment, and changes the campaign before the next wave. Which PDCA phases are most visible?

A. Plan and do only  
B. Check and act  
C. Do and plan  
D. Act only  

**Answer: B**

**Why:** They evaluate results and root cause (check), then change the approach (act).

---

## Question 7

A company offers a discount for customers willing to accept delivery next month rather than this month, when capacity is constrained. This is primarily:

A. demand shaping  
B. demand forecasting  
C. supply measurement  
D. product traceability  

**Answer: A**

**Why:** The company is intentionally changing the timing of customer demand.

---

## Question 8

A manufacturer sells the same spare part through standard distributors and an overnight direct-fulfillment service. Which of the Four Ps is most directly being varied?

A. Product  
B. Price  
C. Place  
D. Promotion  

**Answer: C**

**Why:** The distribution/contact channel and fulfillment method are placement decisions.

---

## Question 9

A mature product still sells well, but several competitors now offer nearly identical performance. Which supply-chain priority is most likely to grow in importance?

A. Ignoring cost because volume is high  
B. Efficiency and service differentiation  
C. Eliminating all inventory immediately  
D. Building unlimited launch capacity  

**Answer: B**

**Why:** Mature products face stronger competition and slower growth, increasing pressure on cost and differentiation.

---

## Question 10

A company is retiring an old industrial controller but must support installed units for seven more years. Which discipline most directly helps manage version information, serial traceability, service documentation, and end-of-life planning?

A. PLM  
B. Price elasticity  
C. Forecast bias  
D. Cross-docking  

**Answer: A**

**Why:** PLM spans product information and support from development through end of life.

---

## Question 11

A new consumer electronics product has highly uncertain demand and is redesigned every year. Which supply-chain approach is most consistent with that situation?

A. Large finished-goods push inventory and long frozen runs  
B. Pull-oriented response, modular design, and short lead times  
C. Maximum standardization with no spare capacity  
D. Fixed high inventory regardless of demand  

**Answer: B**

**Why:** High uncertainty plus high clockspeed calls for responsiveness and protection against obsolescence.

---

## Question 12

A product has stable aggregate demand, but its electronics module changes frequently. What principle is most useful?

A. High clockspeed always requires unstable demand  
B. Modular design can preserve reusable portions across versions  
C. Demand must be fully make-to-order  
D. Product life cycle is irrelevant  

**Answer: B**

**Why:** Stable demand can coexist with high NPI frequency; modularity can separate fast-changing and stable portions.

---

## Question 13

A sales manager lowers next quarter's forecast mainly so the team has a better chance of beating its quota, while the market assumptions are unchanged. What should the demand-review process do?

A. Accept the number because sales owns the customer  
B. Challenge the change and its assumptions  
C. Replace the number with the business-plan target without discussion  
D. Increase safety stock immediately  

**Answer: B**

**Why:** A demand plan should be evidence-based, and unexplained changes should be challenged.

---

## Question 14

A company launches a promotion that increases revenue but the extra orders consume scarce capacity and have negative incremental margin after expedited freight. What was missing from the demand-influencing decision?

A. Focus on contribution and profitability  
B. More branding  
C. A longer product name  
D. A more complex channel  

**Answer: A**

**Why:** Creating volume that reduces profit is not successful demand management.

---

## Question 15

A company can accurately forecast generic modules but cannot predict which final configurations customers will choose. Which approach best fits?

A. Build all finished configurations to forecast  
B. Produce common modules in advance and postpone final configuration until demand is known  
C. Stop forecasting all components  
D. Eliminate product variety  

**Answer: B**

**Why:** This is a classic hybrid push/pull situation where postponement reduces mix risk.
