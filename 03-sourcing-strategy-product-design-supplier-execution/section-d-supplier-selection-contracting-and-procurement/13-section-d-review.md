## Question 1

**Question.** What should be written before supplier proposals arrive?

A. Evaluation gates, criteria, scales, and governance

B. The winner's name

C. The final invoice

D. The press release

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**A. Evaluation gates, criteria, scales, and governance**

### Why it is correct

Predefined criteria reduce bias and preserve fairness.

### Why the other answers are wrong

B, C, and D occur later or predetermine the outcome.

</details>
