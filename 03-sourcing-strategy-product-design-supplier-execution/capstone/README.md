# Rivermark Integrated Sourcing and Design Capstone

## Situation

Rivermark Climate Systems must support growth in modular commercial heat pumps while reducing expedites, supplier concentration, product complexity, and lifecycle cost. Leadership wants one recommendation connecting sourcing, category strategy, product design, supplier award, contract deployment, and procure-to-pay controls.

All organizations, values, and events are fictional.

## Your assignment

1. Recommend make, buy, or hybrid boundaries for controls and assembly; state protected knowledge and transition gates.
2. Reconstruct the control-board cost bridge and identify the assumptions that could reverse the decision.
3. Classify each category and propose relationship and supply-base actions.
4. Compare the three design alternatives, including validation needed before release.
5. Evaluate suppliers without allowing a weighted score to override mandatory gates.
6. Select a negotiation and contract structure for the compressor relationship.
7. Convert at least six obligations into owners, evidence, cadence, thresholds, and responses.
8. Prioritize the open-order exceptions using customer consequence, inventory, recovery cost, and displaced demand.
9. Present a 90-day implementation plan and a one-page executive recommendation.

## Required decision record

For each major recommendation, state:

- decision and owner;
- evidence and assumptions;
- rejected alternative and reason;
- expected benefit and cost;
- residual risk and contingency;
- measure, threshold, and review date.

## Capstone knowledge check

### Question 1

**Question.** Rivermark's $61 distant board becomes $82.60 on a full-cost basis. What should the team do first?

A. Select it because quoted price is lowest

B. Validate the cost bridge and compare service and risk evidence

C. Reject all distant sources

D. Ignore inventory cost

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Validate the cost bridge and compare service and risk evidence**

### Why it is correct

The full comparison must be validated before selecting or rejecting an option.

### Why the other answers are wrong

A and D use incomplete economics; C is an unsupported absolute.

</details>

### Question 2

**Question.** The sensor is low spend but single-source. What is the priority?

A. Automate spot buying

B. Continuity and alternate qualification

C. Reduce inspection immediately

D. Run a price-only auction

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Continuity and alternate qualification**

### Why it is correct

Its supply difficulty and line-stop consequence dominate spend.

### Why the other answers are wrong

A, C, and D do not address continuity.

</details>

**Question.** A supplier scores 78 but fails a mandatory gate. Is it eligible?

A. Yes

B. No

<details>

**B. No**

### Why the other answers are wrong

### Question 4

**Question.** Which design alternative has the strongest combined direction in the fictional dataset?

A. Current integral

B. Modular platform

C. No comparison is possible

D. Choose only by part count

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. Modular platform**

### Why it is correct

It improves parts, assembly, service, pallet density, packaging cost, and estimated failures, subject to validation.

### Why the other answers are wrong

C ignores available evidence; D uses one dimension; A is dominated in the dataset.
