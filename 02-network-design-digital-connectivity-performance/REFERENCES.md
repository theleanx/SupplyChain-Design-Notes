# Module 2 Reference Register

Most concepts in Module 2 are explained from first principles using original examples. The following authoritative public resources support selected standards-related topics.

## Cybersecurity

- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.CSWP.29) — risk-governance structure used in the cybersecurity topic.
- [NIST SP 800-207: Zero Trust Architecture](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-207) — principles for identity-centered access and verification.

## Identification and traceability

- [GS1 barcode standards](https://www.gs1.org/standards/barcodes) — globally interoperable identification and data-capture concepts.
- [GS1 RFID standards](https://www.gs1.org/standards/rfid) — passive RFID and electronic product-code concepts.
- [GS1 traceability standard](https://www.gs1.org/standards/traceability) — identification, capture, and sharing across traceability events.

## Financial reporting

- [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission: Beginner's Guide to Financial Statements](https://www.sec.gov/about/reports-publications/investorpubsbegfinstmtguide) — relationship among the balance sheet, income statement, cash-flow statement, and statement of shareholders' equity.

## Greenhouse-gas accounting

- [GHG Protocol Corporate Standard](https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-standard) — corporate greenhouse-gas inventory principles.
- [GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain Standard](https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-value-chain-scope-3-standard) — upstream and downstream value-chain emissions.

## Use of references

Links provide readers with authoritative follow-up material. No external diagram, table, assessment item, or extended passage is reproduced in this repository.
