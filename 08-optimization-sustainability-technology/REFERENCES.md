## Evidence, edition and freshness cautions

The learning examples' costs, returns, order counts, demand distributions, hypothetical rates, emissions and capacity targets are independent fictional assumptions, not standards. Legal/tax, financial reporting, labor, environmental, reporting and technology requirements can vary by transaction, jurisdiction and time. Current authoritative primary sources must be checked before practical application. Section B will add links to current sustainability standards and their exact versions; Section C will add relevant technology governance references.
