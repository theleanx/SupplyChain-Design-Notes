## Evidence, edition and freshness cautions

The learning examples' costs, returns, order counts, demand distributions, hypothetical rates, emissions and capacity targets are independent fictional assumptions, not standards. Legal/tax, financial reporting, labor, environmental, reporting and technology requirements can vary by transaction, jurisdiction and time. Current authoritative primary sources must be checked before practical application. Current Section B official sustainability standards and editions are covered in its [section-specific source register](./section-b-sustainability/REFERENCES.md). Section C technology, project and governance source mapping appears in its [technology references](./section-c-technology-trends/REFERENCES.md).
