## Public references for verification and further reading

- [ASCM Supply Chain Operations Reference (SCOR) Digital Standard](https://www.ascm.org/corporate-solutions/standards-tools/scor-ds/) — related performance framework, not a risk-certification standard.
- [COSO Enterprise Risk Management](https://www.coso.org/) — verify the currently adopted official ERM edition and component architecture.
- [ISO 31000 — Risk management](https://www.iso.org/iso-31000-risk-management.html) — broad risk-management guidance; adoption does not by itself constitute organization certification.
- [IEC 31010 — Risk management — Risk assessment techniques](https://webstore.iec.ch/) — verify edition/status at the official catalogue before formal use.
- [ISO Guide 73 — Risk management vocabulary](https://www.iso.org/) — verify edition/status at the official catalogue before formal use.
- [ISO 22301 — Business continuity management](https://www.iso.org/standard/75106.html) — course edition 2019; verify current applicability before claiming certification.
- [ISO 28000 — Security and resilience — Security management systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/79612.html) — course edition 2022; verify current applicability before compliance claims.
- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) — cyber-risk control context.
- [WIPO Intellectual Property](https://www.wipo.int/) — authoritative starting point for IP guidance, not transaction-specific legal advice.
- [ICC Incoterms rules](https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/) — verify contractual risk-of-loss rules; do not infer title from Incoterms.

## Use rule

The course references ISO 31000:2018, IEC 31010:2019 (sometimes called ISO 31010), and ISO Guide 73:2009. Those are **course-source labels**, not claims that their editions are current as of publication. Compliance, contracts, insurance and cross-border IP rights require live authoritative review. Case organizations, budgets, thresholds and results in this module are fictional training artifacts, not industry benchmarks.

Section B references the course-listed ISO 22301:2019 and ISO 28000:2022. No conformity or legal conclusion is implied by these descriptions.

**Edition-status checkpoint (2026-10-19):** ISO lists ISO 22301:2019 as published, with a 2024 amendment and a revision under development; ISO 28000:2022 remains published, with a 2024 amendment. This is a catalogue status check only, not certification verification. See the two official links above.
