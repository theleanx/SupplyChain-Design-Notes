# Module 6 Reference Register

Module 6 explanations, examples, datasets, diagrams, calculations, and knowledge checks are independently authored. The public sources below are provided for further exploration of supply-chain performance, customer feedback, supplier quality, and relationship technology/security controls. They do not replace the module's locked fictional training assumptions.

## Use rule

Public standards and guidance can change by edition or revision. For real customer, supplier, certification, cybersecurity, privacy, contract, or compliance decisions, verify the current authoritative version and the transaction-specific facts. The Module 6 weighted scores, certification thresholds, CRM economics, communication thresholds, and capstone gates are fictional repository-defined training controls unless explicitly identified otherwise in the formula/control sheet.
