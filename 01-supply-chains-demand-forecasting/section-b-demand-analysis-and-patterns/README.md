# Section B: Demand Analysis and Patterns

This section explains how a supply-chain professional moves from **"we have a forecast"** to the more useful question **"what forces are shaping demand, and how should the business respond?"**

The section is deliberately split between two horizons:

- **Strategic demand analysis:** external environment, SWOT, market research, competition, global forces, portfolio fit, and economic conditions.
- **Tactical/operational pattern analysis:** trend, seasonality, cycles, promotions, price sensitivity, and random variation.
