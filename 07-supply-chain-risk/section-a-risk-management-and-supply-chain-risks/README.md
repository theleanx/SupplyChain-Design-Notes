# Section A: Risk Management and Supply Chain Risks

Stonebridge Industrial Components is the fictional operating context. This section establishes risk governance and identification before Section B moves into severity analysis, response economics and recovery. The lessons are independently written for this repository; source documents are not reproduced.

## Learning outcomes

- Develop a precise threat or opportunity statement with defined scope, cause, event, effect and horizon.
- Connect enterprise oversight, appetite, tolerances and response authority to actual supply-chain decisions.
- Choose appropriate evidence-gathering and risk-identification methods without false probability precision.
- Maintain an owned, versioned risk register and distinguish physical asset and intangible asset exposures.
- Recognize when safety, compliance, IP or partner continuity requires a non-negotiable review gate.

## How to study the section

First define the event path and objective, then establish the responsible owner and governance limits. Do not introduce quantitative ranking before the scenario and evidence are stable. Move from definitions (A01–A03) to tolerances/plans (A04–A08), then identification/registers (A09–A10) and losses (A11–A12). Complete the original review and retain an unresolved-evidence list. Section B owns probability-impact estimation, response choice, simulation and recovery decisions.
