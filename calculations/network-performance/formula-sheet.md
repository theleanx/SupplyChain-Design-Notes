# Module 2 Formula Sheet

## Decision scoring

$$
\text{Weighted score}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}w_i s_i
$$

Weights should sum to 1.0. Use sensitivity analysis because weights and scores contain judgment.

## Technology investment

$$
\text{Benefit-cost ratio}=\frac{\text{total quantified benefits}}{\text{total quantified costs}}
$$

$$
\text{ROI}=\frac{\text{benefits}-\text{costs}}{\text{costs}}\times100\%
$$

$$
\text{Simple payback}=\frac{\text{initial investment}}{\text{steady annual net cash benefit}}
$$

Simple payback ignores later cash flows and the time value of money.

## Transportation

$$
\text{Weight utilization}=\frac{\text{loaded weight}}{\text{usable weight capacity}}\times100\%
$$

$$
\text{Freight cost per delivered unit}=\frac{\text{total freight cost}}{\text{units delivered}}
$$

## Customer service

$$
\text{Perfect-order rate}=\frac{\text{orders meeting every required condition}}{\text{eligible orders}}\times100\%
$$

Calculate the joint order-level result when possible; do not assume that multiplying aggregate component rates produces the exact value.

## Speed and reliability

$$
\text{Cycle time}=\text{end timestamp}-\text{start timestamp}
$$

Define the start event, end event, calendar treatment, time zone, and exclusions.

## Productivity and margin

$$
\text{Productivity}=\frac{\text{useful accepted output}}{\text{resources consumed}}
$$

$$
\text{Operating margin}=\frac{\text{operating profit}}{\text{revenue}}\times100\%
$$

## Working capital

$$
\text{Cash-to-cash days}=\text{inventory days}+\text{receivable days}-\text{payable days}
$$

$$
\text{Inventory days}=\frac{\text{average inventory}}{\text{annual cost of goods sold}}\times365
$$

$$
\text{Receivable days}=\frac{\text{average trade receivables}}{\text{annual credit revenue}}\times365
$$

$$
\text{Payable days}=\frac{\text{average trade payables}}{\text{annual purchases}}\times365
$$

## Inventory and assets

$$
\text{Inventory turns}=\frac{\text{annual cost of goods sold}}{\text{average inventory at cost}}
$$

$$
\text{Inventory days}\approx\frac{365}{\text{inventory turns}}
$$

$$
\text{Return on operating assets}=\frac{\text{operating profit}}{\text{average operating assets}}
$$

$$
\text{Return on operating assets}=\text{operating margin}\times\text{operating-asset turnover}
$$

## Standard-cost variance

With positive defined as unfavorable:

$$
\text{Price variance}=(\text{actual price}-\text{standard price})\times\text{actual quantity}
$$

$$
\text{Usage variance}=(\text{actual quantity}-\text{standard quantity allowed})\times\text{standard price}
$$

## Financial health indicators

$$
\text{Current ratio}=\frac{\text{current assets}}{\text{current liabilities}}
$$

$$
\text{Quick ratio}=\frac{\text{cash}+\text{marketable securities}+\text{receivables}}{\text{current liabilities}}
$$

$$
\text{Interest coverage}=\frac{\text{earnings before interest and taxes}}{\text{interest expense}}
$$

Ratios are signals that require trend, industry, accounting, and operating context.

## Operational performance

$$
\text{First-pass yield}=\frac{\text{units completing without rework}}{\text{units entering the process}}\times100\%
$$

$$
\text{Schedule attainment}=\frac{\text{planned orders completed within tolerance}}{\text{planned orders due}}\times100\%
$$

$$
\text{Mean time between failures}=\frac{\text{operating time}}{\text{functional failures}}
$$

$$
\text{Mean time to repair}=\frac{\text{corrective repair time}}{\text{repair events}}
$$
