# Sourcing and Procurement Formula Sheet

## Total relevant cost

```text
Total relevant cost = recurring relevant cost + transition cost + expected risk cost − recoverable value
```

Include only costs that differ across alternatives and use a common horizon.

## Landed unit cost

```text
Landed unit cost = purchase price + freight + duty + brokerage + handling + other arrival costs
```

## Total-cost unit equivalent

```text
TCO per unit = present value of relevant lifecycle costs / present value of expected accepted units
```

## Expected risk cost

```text
Expected risk cost = probability of event × financial consequence if event occurs
```

Do not present uncertain estimates as guaranteed cost. Use scenarios when probability or impact is weakly supported.

## Weighted supplier score

```text
Weighted score = Σ(normalized criterion score × criterion weight)
```

Mandatory gates remain separate; a weighted score cannot compensate for infeasibility.

## Supplier concentration

```text
Largest-supplier share = spend or volume with largest supplier / total category spend or volume
HHI = Σ(supplier share²)
```

Use decimal shares for HHI from 0 to 1, or percentage shares for a 0-to-10,000 scale, and label the convention.

## Annualized early-payment discount

```text
Approximate annualized return = discount / (1 − discount) × days per year / (net days − discount days)
```

Use the organization's day-count convention and compare with liquidity and financing alternatives.

## Purchase-price variance

```text
Purchase-price variance = (actual price − comparison price) × actual quantity
```

Label whether the comparison is standard, budget, contract, or prior price.

## On-time delivery

```text
OTD = accepted receipts within the defined window / receipts due in the period
```

Define the reference date, window, partial-delivery rule, and exclusions.

## Defect rate

```text
Defects per million received units = defective accepted-or-screened units / inspected units × 1,000,000
```

Do not compare suppliers with inconsistent inspection or opportunity definitions.
