# Section E: Supply and Demand Alignment / S&OP

Supply and demand alignment turns separate functional plans into **one coordinated operating direction**. This section connects strategy, the business plan, demand planning, production planning, resource planning, finance, and execution through sales and operations planning (S&OP).

The central idea is simple: **sales should sell to a plan that supply can support, supply should produce to the same plan, and finance should confirm that the plan makes business sense.**
