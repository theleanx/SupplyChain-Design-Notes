# Section D — Public References

These public sources are used to keep the regulatory framing current. They supplement the section's supply-chain concepts; they are not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

- **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency — Hazardous Waste Transportation.** Describes RCRA hazardous-waste transportation and the cradle-to-grave manifest framework for applicable off-site shipments.  
  <https://www.epa.gov/hw/hazardous-waste-transportation>

- **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency — e-Manifest.** Current program information, user guidance, and rulemaking updates for the national electronic hazardous-waste manifest system.  
  <https://www.epa.gov/e-manifest>

- **UNECE — Globally Harmonized System of Classification and Labelling of Chemicals (GHS), Rev. 11 (2025).** Current UN GHS publication page; GHS addresses chemical-hazard classification and harmonized labels/SDS elements.  
  <https://unece.org/info/Transport/Dangerous-Goods/pub/407575>

- **European Commission — Waste from Electrical and Electronic Equipment (WEEE).** Overview of EU WEEE policy, collection/treatment/recycling goals, and current evaluation status.  
  <https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/waste-electrical-and-electronic-equipment-weee_en>

- **EUR-Lex — Directive 2012/19/EU on WEEE, consolidated text.** Legal text and later amendments.  
  <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2012/19/2024-04-08/eng>

- **European Commission — RoHS Directive.** Current overview of EU restrictions on hazardous substances in electrical/electronic equipment; the Commission currently lists ten restricted substances/substance groups.  
  <https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/rohs-directive_en>

- **European Commission — RoHS implementation and exemptions.** Current information on exemption procedure and reassessment.  
  <https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/rohs-directive/rohs-directive-implementation_en>

- **Basel Convention — Controlling Transboundary Movements.** Overview of the Convention's control system and movement conditions for hazardous and other covered wastes.  
  <https://www.basel.int/Default.aspx?tabid=4325>

- **Basel Convention — Text of the Convention / e-waste amendments.** Current Convention text page notes that the 2022 e-waste amendments became effective 1 January 2025.  
  <https://www.basel.int/tabid/1275>

## Use rule

For any live transaction, use the current legal text and competent-authority guidance for every jurisdiction in the movement. Do not infer legal waste status, exemptions, producer responsibility, or transboundary permission from this learning material.
