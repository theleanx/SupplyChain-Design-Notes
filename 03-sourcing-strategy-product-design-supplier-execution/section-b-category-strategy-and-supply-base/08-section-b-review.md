# 8. Section B Review

Use these independently authored questions to test decision logic, not term recall. Choose an answer before expanding the rationale.

## Question 1

**Question.** A category contains unrelated markets under one accounting code. What is the problem?

A. Too much competition

B. One strategy cannot sensibly govern the grouped spend

C. The category is too small

D. The currency is fixed

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

### Correct answer

**B. One strategy cannot sensibly govern the grouped spend**

### Why it is correct

Useful categories share drivers, markets, and actions.

### Why the other answers are wrong

A, C, and D do not address category coherence.

</details>

## Question 2
