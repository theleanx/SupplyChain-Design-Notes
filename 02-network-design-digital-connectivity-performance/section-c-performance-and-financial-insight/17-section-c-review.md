# 17. Section C Review and Original Practice

These questions reinforce measurement design, service, flow, cost, assets, sustainability, finance, and operational performance.

## Original knowledge checks

### 1. Metric purpose

What should every material metric lead to?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A defined review, decision, action, or learning process.
</details>

### 2. Balancing measure

What could balance a warehouse productivity target?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Accuracy, order completeness, cycle time, safety, rework, and freight per order.
</details>

### 3. Leading versus lagging

Is perfect-order rate generally an outcome or leading measure?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Generally an outcome measure. Missing confirmations or late milestones may be leading indicators.
</details>

### 4. Dashboard

What primary question does an operational dashboard answer?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

What needs attention now or soon?
</details>

### 5. Scorecard

What primary question does a strategic scorecard answer?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Whether outcomes are progressing toward strategic objectives and what decisions are required.
</details>

### 6. Metric hierarchy

Why assign one end-to-end outcome owner?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The result crosses functions and needs accountable coordination of driver owners and improvement.
</details>

### 7. Benchmark gap

Current performance is 92% and target is 97%. What is the gap?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

5 percentage points.
</details>

### 8. Comparability

Name two checks required before comparing cycle time across companies.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Examples include start/end events, scope, segment mix, geography, service promise, period, and exclusions.
</details>

### 9. Perfect order

Of 2,000 eligible orders, 1,860 meet every condition. What is the perfect-order rate?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`1,860 / 2,000 = 93%`.
</details>

### 10. Promise integrity

Why preserve the original accepted promise after a date change?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

So reliability cannot be improved by moving history and root-cause analysis remains possible.
</details>

### 11. Cycle time

What two timestamps are essential in every cycle-time definition?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The explicit start event and end event.
</details>

### 12. Agility

What is time to recover?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Elapsed time from disruption occurrence to restoration of a defined acceptable performance level.
</details>

### 13. Productivity

A process produces 600 correct units using 150 labor hours. What is labor productivity?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

4 correct units per labor hour.
</details>

### 14. Cash-to-cash

Inventory days are 70, receivable days 40, and payable days 45. What is cash-to-cash time?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`70 + 40 - 45 = 65 days`.
</details>

### 15. Inventory turns

Annual cost of goods sold is $30 million and average inventory is $5 million. What are turns?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

6 turns per year.
</details>

### 16. Sustainability

Why report absolute impact with intensity?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Efficiency may improve while total impact rises because volume or scope changes.
</details>

### 17. Financial statements

Where is inventory generally visible before it is sold: balance sheet or income statement expense?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

As an asset on the balance sheet, subject to applicable accounting policy and valuation adjustments.
</details>

### 18. Price variance

Actual price is $12.50, standard $12.00, and actual quantity 400. What is price variance using positive as unfavorable?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`($12.50-$12.00) × 400 = $200 unfavorable`.
</details>

### 19. Ratio interpretation

Why should supplier ratios be reviewed as trends and with operational evidence?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

One ratio can be distorted by timing, classification, industry, or asset quality and does not prove future failure.
</details>

### 20. Return on operating assets

Operating margin is 12% and asset turnover is 1.5. What is return on operating assets?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`12% × 1.5 = 18%`.
</details>

### 21. First-pass yield

Of 500 units entering a process, 470 pass without rework. What is first-pass yield?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

94%.
</details>

### 22. Maintenance

Operating time is 2,400 hours with six functional failures. What is mean time between failures?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`2,400 / 6 = 400 operating hours` under the stated definition.
</details>
