# Section D: Forecasting

Forecasting turns imperfect information about the future into a usable planning signal. This section focuses on **how to choose a method, remove obvious seasonal distortion, measure error, detect bias, and decide when a forecast model needs attention**.

The goal is not to produce a perfect forecast. The goal is to produce a forecast that is transparent, measurable, useful for planning, and improved over time.
