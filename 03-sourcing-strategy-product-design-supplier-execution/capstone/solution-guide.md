# Rivermark Capstone Solution Guide

There is no single mandatory answer. A strong submission is internally consistent, traceable to the fictional evidence, explicit about uncertainty, and executable.

## 1. Capability boundary

Retain control algorithms, firmware release, test design, interface ownership, and final product release internally. External board assembly is reasonable after process qualification. A hybrid capacity path is defensible where service continuity or learning justifies its additional fixed cost.

## 2. Total-cost conclusion

The distant board's $13 quoted advantage becomes a $3.40 disadvantage in the stated scenario: $82.60 versus $79.20. The largest reversal drivers are purchase price, quality exposure, extra pipeline days, duty, freight, and continuity-control assumptions. Run optimistic and adverse cases rather than declaring the estimate certain.

## 3. Category actions

| Category | Direction |

## 4. Product design

The modular platform leads the fictional dataset across all listed measures, but release requires technical, safety, quality, interface, field-service, and customer validation. Benefits should be separated into recurring savings, one-time conversion cost, working-capital effect, and risk reduction.

## 5. Supplier award

NorthPeak has the highest eligible weighted score at 83. VectorTherm is not eligible despite a score of 78 because a mandatory gate failed. BlueHarbor remains a credible negotiation alternative and possible continuity option.

## 6. Commercial model

For established standard output, use defined pricing with a transparent input-index mechanism where justified. For uncertain joint development, a controlled target-cost or incentive structure can align improvement, provided scope, allowable cost, quality, schedule, audit, and maximum exposure are clear.

## 7. Deployment

Use the provided obligation register as the minimum operating layer. Add contract term, effective date, system control, escalation time, cure path, and record-retention requirement. Test the first forecast, order, receipt, invoice, quality report, and rebate calculation.

## 8. Exceptions

The sensor and compressor exceptions deserve immediate cross-functional review because cover is low and customer or production consequences are high. Validate whether reallocation harms another committed order before approving recovery. Record incremental cost against cause and create prevention actions.

## 9. Ninety-day sequence

- **Days 1–30:** validate data, freeze decision criteria, confirm requirements and capability boundary, initiate alternate-sensor qualification.
- **Days 31–60:** complete design and supplier evidence, negotiate commercial packages, configure obligation and master-data controls.
- **Days 61–90:** run qualification and transaction pilots, test exception escalation, confirm baseline measures, and obtain release approval.

## Executive standard

A persuasive recommendation connects customer outcome, product architecture, supply-market reality, economics, risk, contract mechanism, operating control, and measurable learning. It also states what evidence would cause leadership to change the decision.
