# Integrated Capstone Solution Guide

## Executive Recommendation

State the decision, conditions, residual risk, and implementation gates.

## Reproducible Analysis

For every requested calculation, show the linked source data, formula, substitution,
result, units, interpretation, and decision implication.

## Decision Artifacts

Provide completed comparison tables, control registers, ownership, thresholds, and
evidence requirements rather than describing what a learner should create.

## Sensitivity and Failure Cases

Show at least one plausible case that could change the recommendation and explain the
trigger for reassessment.

## Implementation Roadmap

Separate prerequisites, pilot gates, scale decisions, owners, and outcome measures.

## Evaluation Rubric

Explain what distinguishes incomplete, competent, and excellent submissions.
