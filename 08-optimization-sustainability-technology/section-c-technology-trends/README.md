# Section C: Technology Trends

> **Published Section C — Module 8 v8.0.0, carried forward in v8.0.1.** Includes eighteen original technology/implementation/change/maintenance lessons, eighteen original SVG diagrams, eighteen fictional CSV datasets, and an eighteen-question review. The AlderGate capstone, shared eight-module validator and owner-authorized release have been completed.

Section C follows the privately supplied 2026 CSCP source across emerging technologies, intelligent supply-chain enablers, technology assessment, project management, change management and maintenance. AlderGate Motion Products is an independent fictional case.

## Learning outcomes

- Distinguish cloud SaaS/PaaS/IaaS, AI, machine learning, expert systems, sensors, RFID, telematics and control towers.
- Evaluate IoT, intelligent asset management, digital twins, blockchain, additive manufacturing, AR, wearables, drones, robots, RPA and quantum computing by actual business fit.
- Create sequenced technology roadmaps, audits, risk reviews, strategic/tactical/operational requirements, and gap closure plans.
- Apply vendor TCO and mandatory feasibility gates before ranking alternatives.
- Govern implementation, testing, cutover, project charter, RACI, WBS, schedule and cost baselines.
- Distinguish predictive/agile/hybrid approaches, guide stakeholder change, manage resistance and institutionalize adoption.
- Plan ongoing maintenance and vendor support ownership for critical technology.

## How to study the section

For every topic identify the business promise, technology input/decision boundary, critical feasibility gates, responsible user and partner, implementation acceptance evidence, and funded post-go-live maintenance. Do not declare success from a vendor demo, training attendance or project completion alone.

Complete the [18-question review](./19-section-c-review.md) and its readiness checkpoint before the integrated Module 8 capstone.
