# Module 7: Supply Chain Risk

> **Status: validated release candidate; awaiting final human approval.** Sections A and B and the integrated capstone are fully authored. Stonebridge Industrial Components, datasets, assessment scenarios and thresholds are fictional.

This module adds governance, uncertainty assessment, risk-response decisions and tested continuity to the execution capabilities taught in Modules 1–6. It does not restate those modules' complete planning, procurement, inventory, logistics, customer or supplier methodologies.

## Learning outcomes

- Identify threats, opportunities, shared dependencies, and potentially disruptive supply-chain events.
- Govern risk appetite, tolerance, response budgets, roles and evidence across supply-chain partners.
- Assess probabilities and impacts consistently, with suitable qualitative and optional quantitative techniques.
- Choose feasible responses that meet safety and compliance gates before comparing economic outcomes.
- Develop a tested response and continuity program with accountable recovery and review triggers.

## How each lesson is structured

Each topic contains objectives, plain-language explanation, business relevance, its own original visual, evidence and process flow, a fictional decision scenario, a practical artifact or verified calculation, trade-offs, confusion points, common errors, independently authored assessment with rationales, practitioner application and links to relevant prior-module concepts.
