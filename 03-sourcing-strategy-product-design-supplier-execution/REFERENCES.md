# Module 3 Reference Register

Selected public resources for further exploration are listed below. Module explanations, examples, visuals, datasets, calculations, and knowledge checks are independently authored.

- [ISO 20400 — Sustainable procurement guidance](https://www.iso.org/standard/63026.html)
- [OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Business Conduct](https://mneguidelines.oecd.org/due-diligence-guidance-for-responsible-business-conduct.htm)
- [NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1 — Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/161/r1/final)
- [U.S. GAO Cost Estimating and Assessment Guide](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-20-195g)
- [U.S. International Trade Administration — Trade Finance Guide](https://www.trade.gov/trade-finance-guide)
- [UNCITRAL — United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods](https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/salegoods/conventions/sale_of_goods/cisg)
- [U.S. EPA — Sustainable Materials Management](https://www.epa.gov/smm)
- [ISO 9001 — Quality management systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html)

External standards and laws change. Verify the current authoritative source and obtain qualified legal, financial, trade, tax, quality, cybersecurity, or regulatory advice before applying them.
